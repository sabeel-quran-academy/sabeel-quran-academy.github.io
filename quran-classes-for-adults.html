Designed for busy adults, not classrooms

Many adults feel self-conscious learning to read the Quran later in life, especially converts and reverts, or those raised without formal Islamic education. Our tutors specialise in one-on-one adult learning — no group setting, no comparison to other students.

  • Complete beginner track — start from the Arabic alphabet, at your own pace.
  • Fluency & Tajweed refinement — for adults who can already read but want to improve pronunciation.
  • Flexible scheduling — early morning before work, lunch breaks, or after Isha.
  • Private and pressure-free — mistakes are part of learning; our tutors are trained for adult learners specifically.

Popular with new Muslims and busy professionals

We regularly teach new Muslims (reverts/converts) across the USA, UK, Canada and Europe who want a calm introduction to reading the Quran without assumptions about prior knowledge. We also work with professionals in the UAE and Saudi Arabia fitting classes around demanding work schedules.

Every adult student gets a private WhatsApp line to their tutor for questions between classes.

Your learning path as an adult student

1. Level assessment

Your free trial class tells us whether to start with Arabic letters, Noorani Qaida, or straight into Nazra/Tajweed refinement.

2. A realistic weekly schedule

Most adults manage 2-3 classes a week of 30-45 minutes — enough for steady progress without burnout.

3. Ongoing Tajweed correction

Once reading is fluent, classes shift focus to correct articulation (Makhraj) and recitation rules.

Frequently asked questions

I'm a complete beginner as an adult — can you really teach me?

Yes. A large share of our adult students, including new Muslims, start from the Arabic alphabet with no prior reading ability.

How long does it take an adult to learn to read the Quran?

Most adult beginners reach basic fluent reading within 4-8 months with 2-3 classes a week, though pace varies by individual.

Can I take classes early in the morning or late at night?

Yes, we offer early morning, lunch-hour and late evening slots to fit around work schedules across UK, US, Canadian and Gulf time zones.

Is it embarrassing to learn as an adult with a live tutor?

Our tutors work exclusively with adult learners in one-on-one settings and are trained to teach without judgment — there is no classroom audience.
