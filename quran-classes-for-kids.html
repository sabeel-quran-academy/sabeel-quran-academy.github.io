A curriculum built for young learners

Our kids' program moves at a pace matched to your child's age and attention span, using short, focused sessions rather than long lectures.

  • Noorani Qaida — Arabic letter recognition and joining, the foundation for reading the Quran.
  • Nazra Quran — fluent Quran reading with correct pronunciation.
  • Basic Tajweed — introduced gradually once reading is fluent.
  • Islamic Studies — short duas, akhlaq (manners), and stories of the Prophets, age-appropriate.

What parents in the UK, USA, Canada & the Gulf ask us most

Parents raising children outside a Muslim-majority country often worry their kids won't get consistent, quality Quran teaching. Our tutors work specifically with children growing up in the UK, USA, Canada, UAE and Saudi Arabia, understanding school schedules and time zones on both sides.

Classes are recorded homework-free — instead, tutors send a short voice note recap so parents can follow progress without extra screen time.

How we keep young students engaged

Short, consistent sessions

20–30 minute classes, 3–5 times a week, are more effective for children than long, infrequent lessons.

Same tutor every class

Your child keeps the same teacher throughout, building comfort and consistency rather than starting over each time.

Positive reinforcement, not pressure

Tutors are trained to encourage rather than scold — especially important for children learning a second script (Arabic) alongside English or Urdu.

Frequently asked questions

What age can my child start online Quran classes?

We accept children from age 4, though most structured Noorani Qaida programs work best from age 5–6 onward.

How long is each class?

Kids' classes typically run 20–30 minutes, several times a week, to match a young learner's attention span.

Will my child have the same tutor every time?

Yes, we assign one consistent tutor per student so your child builds familiarity and trust over time.

Do you teach girls with female tutors only?

You can request a female tutor for your daughter when booking, and we will match accordingly wherever available.
