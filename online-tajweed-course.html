Why Tajweed matters beyond "reading correctly"

Tajweed isn't just pronunciation — small mistakes in Makhraj or Ghunnah can change the meaning of a word. Our Tajweed course focuses on live, real-time correction, since this is something recorded courses and apps cannot do.

  • Makhraj — the correct articulation point for every Arabic letter.
  • Sifaat — the characteristics that distinguish similar-sounding letters.
  • Rules of Noon & Meem Sakinah — Ikhfa, Idgham, Iqlab and Izhar.
  • Madd rules — correct elongation lengths during recitation.

Who this course is for

  • Students who can already read the Quran but want to correct long-standing pronunciation habits.
  • Adults preparing to lead prayer (Imamat) or recite publicly.
  • Hifz students who want stronger Tajweed foundations before or during memorization.
  • Anyone preparing for a Quran recitation Ijazah.

Course structure

Assessment class

Your tutor listens to your current recitation and identifies specific Makhraj and rule-based corrections needed.

Rule-by-rule lessons

Each Tajweed rule is taught with examples directly from the Quran, then practiced live until applied correctly and consistently.

Applied recitation practice

Once rules are learned individually, sessions shift to full-surah recitation with real-time correction.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to already read Quran fluently to join the Tajweed course?

Basic reading ability is required; the course focuses on correcting and refining pronunciation rather than teaching letters from scratch.

How long does it take to learn Tajweed properly?

Most students see clear improvement within 2-3 months of consistent classes, though mastery of all rules typically takes 6-12 months.

Can Tajweed really be taught properly online?

Yes — live video allows the tutor to hear articulation in real time and correct it immediately, the same as an in-person class.

Is this course suitable for someone preparing to lead prayer?

Yes, many students join specifically to refine recitation before leading Taraweeh or daily prayers.
