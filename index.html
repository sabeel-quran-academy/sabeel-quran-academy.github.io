Why families choose online Quran classes over a local madrasa

For Muslim families in the UK, USA, Canada, UAE, Saudi Arabia and Pakistan, finding a qualified, patient Quran teacher nearby isn't always possible. Online Quran classes remove that barrier completely.

  • One-on-one attention — no sharing a tutor with 15 other students.
  • Choose your tutor — request a male or female teacher, and a preferred accent or teaching style.
  • Flexible timing — classes scheduled around school, work and salah times, across time zones.
  • Progress you can see — every class logged, with monthly progress reports for parents.

How your first class works

1. Book a free trial class

Tell us the student's age, current Quran reading level, and preferred class times. We'll match you with a suitable tutor within 24 hours.

2. Meet your tutor on a live video call

A short assessment class over Zoom or Google Meet, so the tutor can plan the right starting point — Noorani Qaida, Nazra, Tajweed or Hifz.

3. Start your regular class schedule

Continue with 2–5 classes a week at a fixed time. Missed classes can be rescheduled, and progress is reviewed every month.

Frequently asked questions

How much do online Quran classes cost?

Pricing depends on your country, the number of weekly classes and whether you choose one-on-one or group classes. Visit our locations page for country-specific plans, or book a free trial to get a personalised quote.

Can I choose a female tutor for my daughter?

Yes. When you book your free trial, tell us your preference for a male or female tutor and we'll match you accordingly.

What app or software do you use for online classes?

Classes run over Zoom or Google Meet with a shared digital Quran/Qaida on screen, so both tutor and student can follow along in real time.

Do you teach adults with no prior Quran reading experience?

Yes, many of our adult students start from zero. See our adult Quran classes page for how the beginner track works.

What time zones do you cover?

We schedule classes across UK, European, Gulf, Pakistan and North American time zones, including early morning and late evening slots.
