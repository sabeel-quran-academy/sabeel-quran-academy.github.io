The Sabaq–Sabqi–Manzil method

Rushed memorization without proper revision is the most common reason students lose what they've memorized. Our Hifz tutors follow the traditional three-part method in every session:

  • Sabaq — today's new portion to memorize, corrected line by line.
  • Sabqi — recent revision, usually the last 5-7 days of new memorization.
  • Manzil — long-term revision of everything memorized so far, to prevent forgetting.

This keeps memorization permanent rather than temporary — the biggest concern parents raise when starting Hifz.

Hifz for kids, teenagers and adults

We run separate tracks depending on the student's age and goals:

  • Full-time Hifz — for students dedicating most of their day to memorization, with 2-3 sessions daily.
  • Part-time Hifz — for school-going children and working adults, 1 session a day around other commitments.
  • Short surah memorization — for adults who want to memorize select surahs rather than complete the full Quran.

How progress is tracked

Daily class log

Every Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil portion is logged after each class, so nothing is missed.

Monthly Hifz report

Parents receive a monthly summary showing total Quran memorized, accuracy, and revision consistency.

Ijazah pathway

Advanced students working toward completing full Hifz are guided toward a formal Ijazah (certification) route with an approved Qari.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to complete Hifz-ul-Quran online?

Full-time students often complete Hifz in 2-4 years; part-time students combining Hifz with school or work typically take longer, depending on daily memorization pace.

Does my child need to read fluently before starting Hifz?

Yes, we recommend completing Nazra (fluent Quran reading) first, so memorization is built on correct pronunciation from the start.

What happens if a student forgets memorized portions?

This is exactly why the Manzil (long-term revision) portion exists in every class — consistent revision is built into the schedule, not left to the student alone.

Can adults start a Hifz program too?

Yes, we support adult Hifz students, including part-time schedules and short-surah memorization goals rather than the complete Quran.
